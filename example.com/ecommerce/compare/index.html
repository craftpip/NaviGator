Our Verdict

Best overall: NovaLite 5 — It leads in benchmarks, charging speed, battery life, display refresh rate, and RAM, while costing $350 less than the ProMax Ultra. The 5-year update guarantee and full AI feature suite make it future-proof. The only real gaps are mmWave 5G and satellite SOS, which most users won't miss.

Best for photographers: ProMax Ultra — If camera quality is your top priority, the 200MP sensor with 5× optical zoom and OIS on every lens is unmatched. The titanium build is also more durable. But you pay a premium for these advantages.

Best for early adopters: PixelFold — The foldable form factor is genuinely useful for multitasking and media consumption. The 7-year update guarantee and Gemini Nano AI are best-in-class. But the battery life, charging speed, and performance compromises are significant. Worth it only if the foldable form factor is non-negotiable.
