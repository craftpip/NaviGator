Role descriptions

Admin — Full workspace control. Can manage billing, invite/remove members, delete projects, and configure all settings. Assign to co-founders and team leads.
Member — Can deploy, view logs, manage environment variables, and create projects. Cannot access billing or manage members. Default for engineers.
Viewer — Read-only access to dashboards, logs, and deployment history. Great for stakeholders, clients, and QA reviewers.
