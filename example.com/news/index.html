Local Library Reopens After Six-Month Renovation

By News Desk · Published 2026-08-02 · Updated 2026-08-03

TESTVILLE — The Central Testville Library reopened its doors on Monday after a six-month renovation that added a new reading hall, a children's wing, and a digital media lab.

Hundreds of residents lined up outside the century-old building for the ribbon-cutting ceremony. The mayor called the reopening "a new chapter" for the community and praised the library staff for working through the construction.

What changed

  • Two new floors of reading space
  • A dedicated children's area with story-time rooms
  • A digital media lab with free computers
  • Extended evening hours from 8am to 10pm
"The building is newer, but the mission is the same." — Head Librarian Jane Doe

The library is open Monday through Saturday from 8am to 10pm and Sundays from 10am to 6pm. Entry is free and no membership is required to browse.
