Testville Parks ✓ @testvilleparks · 3d
🧵 THREAD: The story of the Testville Geese. A thread. 🧵

1/7 — In 2019, three Canada geese landed in Central Park pond. We named them Harold, Kevin, and Nancy. They were supposed to stay two weeks.
💬 67 🔁 412 ❤️ 3,200 📊
2/7 — By month two, they had claimed the entire pond. Harold bit a jogger. Nancy laid three eggs. Kevin just stared at people.
💬 8🔁 34❤️ 456📊
3/7 — We hired a wildlife consultant. She said, "You don't remove geese. The geese remove you." We didn't understand then.
💬 12🔁 45❤️ 523📊
4/7 — In 2020, the geese population exploded. 12 geese. Then 30. They held a "meeting" near the fountain every morning at 6am. We have no evidence of agenda.
💬 19🔁 78❤️ 890📊
5/7 — The council debated removing them. The motion failed 5-4 after Harold showed up to the meeting and sat in the gallery. No one could focus.
💬 23🔁 112❤️ 1,340📊
6/7 — By 2023, we gave up and built them a proper habitat. A pond island, native grasses, a sign that says "Goose Crossing." They seemed pleased.
💬 8🔁 56❤️ 678📊
7/7 — Today there are 47 geese in Central Park. They outnumber the staff 3:1. Harold is still biting joggers. Nancy is a grandmother. Kevin still just stares. 🪿
💬 45🔁 1,200❤️ 5,400📊
